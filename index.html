Structure

Four phases, in order

9–12 months part-time at 10–15 hours a week. 5–6 months full-time. The order matters — you cannot build a design system before you understand what an approval chain is.

Phase 1 · Wk 1–8

Foundations

Research, information architecture, and low-fidelity wireframing with real data.

Phase 2 · Wk 9–16

Tool mastery

Figma fluency, Auto Layout that survives resizing, and a tokenized design system.

Phase 3 · Wk 17–28

Specialisation

Dashboard patterns, data visualization, responsive rules, accessibility, testing.

Phase 4 · Wk 29–36

Professional practice

Handoff, domain knowledge, and defending decisions in business terms.

Output

Build four pieces. Depth beats breadth.

Four deep case studies outperform twelve shallow ones. Skip the unsolicited redesign of a famous consumer app — every reviewer has seen three hundred of them.

Case study structure

Ten sections, in this order

    Avoid

    What gets you rejected

    • Unsolicited redesigns of Spotify, Netflix, or Instagram
    • Dribbble-style dashboards with six charts and no task
    • "I made it purple" as design rationale
    • Screens with no error, empty, or loading states
    • No mention of constraints, stakeholders, or trade-offs
    • Beautiful mockups with no evidence anyone could use them

    Assignments

    Five that actually work

      Grading rubric

      What to weight

      • 25% — Workflow understanding. Can they explain the business process?
      • 20% — State coverage. Empty, loading, error, partial, permission-denied.
      • 20% — System consistency. Tokens used, components reused, no one-offs.
      • 20% — Accessibility. Contrast, keyboard, semantics, chart alternatives.
      • 15% — Handoff quality. Could a developer build it without asking?

      Visual polish: 0%. It will improve anyway. Nothing else will unless you grade it.

      Self-assessment

      Milestone checklist

      Ten capabilities. When you can do all ten, you are not a junior who knows Figma — you are a designer a business will trust with its operations.

      Understand the workflow before you design the interface.

      Every senior enterprise designer has the same habit: they can explain the business process from memory before they open Figma. That is the whole differentiator. Everything else in this guide is scaffolding for that one capability.
